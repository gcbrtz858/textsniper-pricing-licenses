## What users say, and where TextSniper falls short

On Software Advice, reviewers describe TextSniper as a tool with no learning curve. The points that come up repeatedly are the menubar launch, the small install size (one reviewer measured 9.8 MB), the speed, and the fact that it slots into the macOS screenshot and clipboard workflow. One reviewer uses it to capture notes during video calls; another wires it into a GTD setup with OmniFocus and Reminders. The text-to-speech option and the built-in QR code reader get mentioned as bonuses rather than the main event. On TrustRadius it holds an 8.2 out of 10, with small businesses as the main user group.

The complaints are consistent too, and they are worth knowing before you pay.

**Punctuation gets dropped.** One reviewer notes that indentation and punctuation marks are not captured reliably, specifically quotes and brackets. If you are pulling code snippets or legal text where a missing quotation mark changes the meaning, budget time for cleanup.

**There are no app integrations.** The same reviewers who like the app ask for direct push to Notion, Coda, or Readwise. Today the output goes to your clipboard, and whatever you do after that is your own workflow.

**The license is per Mac.** This bites people who assume a one-time purchase covers all their devices. It does not. The single-Mac license covers one machine, which is why the three-Mac tier exists.

**Upgrade policy is not spelled out.** TextSniper is sold as a one-time purchase, but the license pages I checked do not state whether future major versions are included. If you plan to keep the app for five years, ask support before you buy rather than assuming.

**Buying through a reseller changes who handles what.** The link in this article goes to Lizhi Shop, a software license reseller rather than the developer's own store. Two things are worth confirming on the product page before you pay: how the license is delivered (a key you enter in the app, or something tied to an account) and what refund window the reseller offers.
